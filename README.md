# iHateConstruction

**The smart way to estimate construction materials and costs.**

A platform that helps contractors, builders, and project managers get accurate material estimates in seconds — no spreadsheets, no guessing, no math errors.

## The Problem We Solve

Construction estimation is broken:
- Manual calculations are slow and error-prone
- Spreadsheets are confusing and get lost
- Quotes take days to prepare
- Material costs change by location, but you use old data
- No way to compare projects side-by-side

**iHateConstruction fixes this.**

## What You Get

### Instant Calculators (Free, No Signup)
Run material estimates in seconds:
- **Brick Calculator** — How many bricks and cement do you need?
- **Concrete Calculator** — What's the exact cement-sand-aggregate mix?
- **Steel Calculator** — Calculate reinforcement weight and cost
- **Paint Calculator** — How much paint and putty for the walls?
- **Tile Calculator** — Tiles, adhesive, grout — all calculated

Get results immediately. Share with clients. No account required.

### Smart Projects (Sign In)
Save your work and track everything:
- Store calculation results for each project
- Track material prices you actually pay
- Auto-populate material lists from your calculations
- Generate professional bills of quantities for quotations

### Bills of Quantities (Coming Soon)
Turn calculations into professional documents:
- Structured breakdowns of every material needed
- Automatic cost calculations
- Adjust quantities or prices anytime
- Export for quotations and client proposals
