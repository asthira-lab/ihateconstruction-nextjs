## What is a concrete foundation?

A concrete foundation is the underground part of a building that carries its weight into the soil. The most common type for houses and low walls is a **continuous strip foundation**: a wide, shallow concrete footing poured in a trench along the full run of the wall, topped by a **stem wall** that rises to just above ground level. Because the concrete is ordered by **volume**, you need the footing's length × width × depth plus the stem wall's length × thickness × height.

This calculator turns those dimensions into the three things you actually order: **cubic metres or cubic yards** of ready-mix, the **number of premix bags** (80 lb or 60 lb), and the **ready-mix truck loads** you'll need.

## How to use the concrete foundation calculator

1. Enter the **run length** — the total length of footing trench under the wall (or walls).
2. Enter the **footing width** and **footing depth** from your foundation drawings. Typical continuous footings are 300–600 mm wide and 200–300 mm deep.
3. Tick **include a stem wall** and enter its **height** and **thickness** if your design has one — most do.
4. Adjust the **wastage** for over-dig, spillage, and formwork — 5% is a sensible default.
5. Press **Calculate** and read off the volume, bag count, weight, and truck count.

## Worked example

A 10 m strip foundation, 0.45 m wide and 0.3 m deep, with a 0.23 m × 0.6 m stem wall:

- Footing = 10 × 0.45 × 0.3 = **1.35 m³**
- Stem wall = 10 × 0.23 × 0.6 = **1.38 m³**
- Total = **2.73 m³** (≈ 3.57 yd³)
- With 5% wastage ≈ **2.87 m³** (3.75 yd³)
- 80 lb premix bags: 2.87 m³ ÷ 0.017 m³ per bag ≈ **169 bags**
- Ready-mix: 3.75 yd³ ÷ 7 yd³ per truck ≈ **0.5 of a truck**

That size suits a single-storey extension. A larger house might run 30–50 m of footing, pushing the order well over a full 7 yd³ load.

## Notes and caveats

- The volume is calculated from **nominal trench and wall dimensions**. Real trenches over-dig, formwork bulges, and the compacted bed eats into the depth, so always order 5–10% extra.
- Bag counts assume a standard premix yield of **0.6 ft³ per 80 lb bag** and **0.45 ft³ per 60 lb bag**. Actual yield varies by brand and mixing.
- Weight uses a **2400 kg/m³** density, typical for standard reinforced concrete.
- This calculator sizes the concrete for the footing and stem wall only. It does **not** include slab-on-grade floors, grade beams, piles, or the gravel bed, vapour barrier, and reinforcement.
- Footing width and depth are structural decisions set by the wall load and the soil's bearing capacity — and by the frost line in cold climates. Confirm them with a structural engineer; the numbers here are for ordering materials, not for structural design.
