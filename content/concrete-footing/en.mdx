## What is a concrete footing?

A concrete footing spreads the weight of a wall or column onto the ground so it doesn't sink or crack. Every foundation starts with one — a strip of concrete under a masonry wall, a pad under a porch post, or a round base under a deck pier. Because footings are poured in trenches or forms, you order concrete by **volume**: the footing's footprint times its depth.

This calculator turns your footing type and dimensions into the three things you actually order: **cubic metres or cubic yards** of ready-mix, the **number of premix bags** (80 lb or 60 lb), and the **ready-mix truck loads** you'll need — for a single footing or a run of identical ones.

## Types of concrete footings

The calculator supports the three most common shapes:

- **Continuous (wall strip) footing** — a long, shallow prism poured under an entire wall. Define it by length × width × height.
- **Spread (isolated pad) footing** — a square or rectangular pad under a single post or column. Define it by length × width × height, then set the quantity for however many identical pads you need.
- **Circular (pier) footing** — a cylinder under a round post or pier. Define it by diameter × height, with quantity for identical piers.

## How to use the concrete footing calculator

1. Pick the **footing type** that matches your foundation.
2. Enter the dimensions shown for that type — length, width, and height (or diameter and height for piers). Any unit works: metres, feet, centimetres, inches, or millimetres.
3. Set the **quantity** to the number of identical footings you're pouring.
4. Adjust the **wastage** for over-dig, spillage, and formwork — 5% is a sensible default.
5. Press **Calculate** and read off the volume, bag count, weight, and truck count.

## Worked example

A continuous footing under a 10 m masonry wall, 0.5 m wide and 0.3 m thick:

- Volume = 10 × 0.5 × 0.3 = **1.5 m³** (≈ 2.0 yd³)
- With 5% wastage ≈ **1.58 m³** (2.06 yd³)
- 80 lb premix bags: 1.58 m³ ÷ 0.0222 yd³ per bag ≈ **88 bags**
- Ready-mix: 2.06 yd³ ÷ 7 yd³ per truck ≈ **0.3 of a truck**

For a row of eight spread pads at 0.6 m × 0.6 m × 0.3 m each: 8 × 0.6 × 0.6 × 0.3 = **0.86 m³** (1.13 yd³) before wastage — roughly a quarter of a 7 yd³ load, or about 51 bags of 80 lb premix.

## Notes and caveats

- The volume is calculated from **nominal dimensions** and ignores the compacted-bed over-dig and the space lost to formwork, so always order 5–10% extra.
- Bag counts assume a standard premix yield of **0.6 ft³ per 80 lb bag** and **0.45 ft³ per 60 lb bag**. Actual yield varies by brand and mixing.
- Weight uses a **2400 kg/m³** density, typical for standard reinforced concrete.
- This calculator sizes the concrete for the footing body only. It does **not** include the concrete or rebar in attached walls, grade beams, or piles, nor the gravel bed, vapour barrier, or reinforcement.
- Footing size is a structural decision: width and depth depend on the wall or column load and the soil's bearing capacity. Confirm with a structural engineer — the numbers here are for ordering materials, not for structural design.
