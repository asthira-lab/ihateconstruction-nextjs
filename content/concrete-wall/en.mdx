## What is a concrete wall?

A concrete wall is any vertical wall cast in concrete — a basement wall, retaining wall, garden boundary, or structural partition. Whether you're pouring it with formwork or filling blocks, you order concrete by **volume**: the wall's face area (length × height) times its thickness. Get that number wrong and you either run short mid-pour or pay for a truck you didn't need.

This calculator turns length, height, and thickness into the three things you actually order: **cubic metres or cubic yards** of ready-mix, the **number of premix bags** (80 lb or 60 lb), and the **ready-mix truck loads** you'll need.

## How to use the concrete wall calculator

1. Enter the **length** of the wall — the full run, ignoring openings for now. Any unit works: metres, feet, centimetres, inches, or millimetres.
2. Enter the **height** of the wall from footing or ground level to the top.
3. Enter the **thickness** of the wall. Partition walls are typically 100–150 mm (4–6 in); basement and retaining walls run 200–300 mm (8–12 in).
4. Adjust the **wastage** for formwork spill, uneven faces, and over-pour. 5% is a sensible default.
5. Press **Calculate** and read off the volume, bag count, weight, and truck count.

## Worked example

A 10 m retaining wall, 3 m high and 150 mm thick:

- Face area = 10 × 3 = 30 m²
- Thickness = 150 mm = 0.15 m
- Volume = 30 × 0.15 = **4.5 m³** (≈ 5.9 yd³)
- With 5% wastage ≈ **4.73 m³** (6.18 yd³)
- 80 lb premix bags: 4.73 m³ ÷ 0.0222 yd³ per bag ≈ **278 bags**
- Ready-mix: 6.18 yd³ ÷ 7 yd³ per truck ≈ **0.9 of a truck**

For a smaller garden wall, a 6 m run at 1.8 m high and 100 mm thick works out to 1.08 m³ — about 1.4 yd³, which is roughly a fifth of a single 7 yd³ ready-mix load.

## Notes and caveats

- The volume uses the **nominal wall envelope** — it does **not** deduct doors, windows, or other openings. For walls with large openings, subtract the opening volumes or the estimate will run high.
- It also does **not** include the footing the wall sits on, the concrete for columns or pilasters, or the cavity in a cavity wall — add those volumes separately.
- Bag counts assume a standard premix yield of **0.6 ft³ per 80 lb bag** and **0.45 ft³ per 60 lb bag**. Actual yield varies by brand and how wet you mix it.
- Weight uses a **2400 kg/m³** density, typical for standard reinforced concrete.
- For retaining and structural walls, confirm the wall thickness, reinforcement, and waterproofing with a structural engineer. The numbers here are for ordering materials, not for structural design.
