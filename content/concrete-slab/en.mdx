## What is a concrete slab?

A concrete slab is a flat, horizontal slab of concrete that forms the floor of a house, garage, shed, patio, or driveway. Pouring one means ordering concrete by **volume** — calculated from the slab's footprint (length × width) times its thickness. Get that number wrong and you either run short mid-pour or overpay for a truck you didn't need.

This calculator turns length, width, and thickness into the three things you actually order: **cubic metres or cubic yards** of ready-mix, the **number of premix bags** (80 lb or 60 lb), and the **ready-mix truck loads** you'll need.

## How to use the concrete slab calculator

1. Enter the **length** and **width** of the slab footprint. Any unit works — metres, feet, centimetres, inches, or millimetres.
2. Enter the **thickness** of the slab. Typical residential slabs are 100–150 mm (4–6 in); driveways and garage floors usually run 150 mm (6 in).
3. Adjust the **wastage** if you expect spillage, formwork over-dig, or a bumpy subgrade. 5% is a sensible default for a flat site.
4. Press **Calculate** and read off the volume, bag count, and truck count.

## Worked example

A 10 ft × 10 ft garage slab, 4 in thick:

- Area = 100 ft²
- Thickness = 4 in = 0.333 ft
- Volume = 100 × 0.333 = 33.3 ft³ ≈ **1.23 yd³** (0.94 m³)
- With 5% wastage ≈ **1.30 yd³** (0.99 m³)
- 80 lb premix bags: 1.30 yd³ ÷ 0.0222 yd³ per bag ≈ **58 bags**
- Ready-mix: 1.30 yd³ ÷ 7 yd³ per truck ≈ **0.2 of a truck**

For a bigger pour, a 6 m × 4 m slab at 150 mm thick works out to 3.6 m³ — about 4.7 yd³, which is roughly two-thirds of a single 7 yd³ ready-mix load.

## Notes and caveats

- The volume is calculated from **nominal dimensions** — it assumes a perfectly flat slab of uniform thickness. Real subgrades settle, formwork bulges, and screeding eats into the top, so always order 5–10% extra.
- Bag counts assume a standard premix yield of **0.6 ft³ per 80 lb bag** and **0.45 ft³ per 60 lb bag**. Actual yield varies by brand and how wet you mix it.
- Weight uses a **2400 kg/m³** density, typical for standard reinforced concrete.
- This calculator sizes the concrete for the slab body only. It does **not** include footing beams, haunches, thickening under load-bearing walls, or concrete for columns — add those volumes separately.
- For structural slabs, confirm mix grade and reinforcement with a structural engineer. The numbers here are for ordering materials, not for structural design.
